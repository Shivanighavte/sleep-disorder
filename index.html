Sleep Tips & Recommendations

Evidence-based strategies for better sleep health

Circadian Rhythm
Maintain a Consistent Sleep Schedule
Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day — even on weekends. This reinforces your body's natural sleep-wake cycle and improves overall sleep quality within 2–3 weeks.
Environment
Optimize Your Sleep Environment
Keep your bedroom cool (65–68°F / 18–20°C), dark, and quiet. Use blackout curtains, white noise machines, or earplugs. Reserve your bed only for sleep — not work or screens.
Light Exposure
Manage Light Exposure
Get bright light in the morning to set your circadian clock. Avoid blue light from screens 1–2 hours before bed. Consider blue-light blocking glasses or enabling Night Mode on devices.
Pre-Sleep Routine
Create a Wind-Down Ritual
Spend 30–60 minutes before bed doing calming activities: light reading, gentle stretching, meditation, or a warm bath. A consistent pre-sleep routine signals your brain that it's time to sleep.
Nutrition
Watch What You Eat & Drink
Avoid caffeine after 2 PM and heavy meals within 3 hours of bedtime. Limit alcohol — it may help you fall asleep but disrupts REM sleep. Magnesium-rich foods (nuts, seeds, leafy greens) can aid sleep.
Napping
Nap Strategically
If you need to nap, keep it to 20–30 minutes and before 3 PM. Short "power naps" boost alertness without disrupting nighttime sleep. Longer naps can cause sleep inertia and nighttime wakefulness.
CBT-I
Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia
CBT-I is the gold-standard treatment for chronic insomnia — more effective than sleeping pills with no side effects. It includes sleep restriction, stimulus control, and cognitive restructuring. Ask your doctor for a referral.
Stimulus Control
Strengthen the Bed-Sleep Association
Only go to bed when sleepy. If you can't sleep after 20 minutes, get up and do something calm in dim light. Return to bed only when sleepy. This retrains your brain to associate bed with sleep, not wakefulness.
Worry Time
Schedule Your Worries
Set aside 15–20 minutes in the afternoon to write down worries and action plans. When anxious thoughts arise at night, remind yourself you've already addressed them. This reduces nighttime rumination significantly.
Relaxation
Progressive Muscle Relaxation
Systematically tense and release muscle groups from toes to head. This reduces physical tension and activates the parasympathetic nervous system. Practice it nightly for 2 weeks to see significant improvement.
Sleep Log
Track Your Sleep Patterns
Keep a daily sleep diary for 2 weeks: bedtime, wake time, sleep quality (1–10), naps, caffeine, exercise. Patterns often emerge that reveal specific triggers. This data is invaluable for your doctor or sleep therapist.
Paradoxical Intention
Try to Stay Awake
Paradoxically, trying to stay awake while lying in bed (without screens) can reduce sleep anxiety. This technique from CBT-I reduces performance anxiety around sleep and often helps people fall asleep faster.
Treatment
CPAP Therapy Is Highly Effective
Continuous Positive Airway Pressure (CPAP) therapy is the most effective treatment for moderate-to-severe sleep apnea. Modern machines are quiet and comfortable. Consistent use dramatically improves sleep quality, energy, and cardiovascular health.
Position
Sleep on Your Side
Sleeping on your back worsens sleep apnea by allowing the tongue and soft tissues to collapse into the airway. Side sleeping (especially left side) keeps the airway open. Use a body pillow or positional device to maintain the position.
Weight
Weight Management Is Critical
Even 10% body weight reduction can reduce apnea severity by 50% in overweight individuals. Excess fat around the neck and throat narrows the airway. Diet and exercise are powerful adjuncts to CPAP therapy.
Alcohol & Sedatives
Avoid Alcohol Before Bed
Alcohol and sedatives relax the throat muscles, significantly worsening sleep apnea. Even one drink within 4 hours of bedtime can increase apnea events by 25%. This is one of the most impactful lifestyle changes you can make.
Throat Exercises
Myofunctional Therapy
Oropharyngeal exercises (tongue, soft palate, and throat muscle training) can reduce sleep apnea severity by 39% and snoring by 59%. These exercises take 15–20 minutes daily and show results within 3 months.
Monitoring
Regular Follow-Up Is Essential
Sleep apnea is a chronic condition requiring ongoing management. Schedule annual sleep studies, monitor your CPAP data (AHI, mask leak, usage hours), and report daytime sleepiness or new symptoms to your sleep specialist.
Exercise
Exercise Regularly, But Time It Right
Regular aerobic exercise improves sleep quality by 65% and reduces time to fall asleep. Aim for 150 minutes per week. Finish vigorous exercise at least 3 hours before bed, as it temporarily raises core temperature and alertness.
Stress Management
Lower Cortisol Before Bed
Chronic stress elevates cortisol, which directly suppresses melatonin production. Practice daily stress-reduction: 10 minutes of mindfulness meditation, journaling, yoga, or deep breathing (4-7-8 technique) to activate your parasympathetic system.
Screen Time
Digital Sunset Protocol
Blue light from screens suppresses melatonin by up to 50%. Implement a "digital sunset" 90 minutes before bed. If unavoidable, use blue-light filters and dim screens. Try replacing evening screen time with reading or podcasts.
Hydration
Stay Hydrated Throughout the Day
Dehydration worsens snoring and sleep apnea by making tissues stickier. Aim for 8 glasses of water daily, mostly before 6 PM to reduce nocturia. Limit fluid intake in the 2 hours before bed to minimize nighttime waking.
Occupation
Manage Work-Related Sleep Risks
Our model found Occupation is the 3rd most important predictor (13.5%). High-stress jobs (nurses, doctors, sales) have higher disorder rates. Set work boundaries, avoid checking email after hours, and use work-to-home transition rituals.
Sunlight
Morning Sunlight Is Medicine
10–30 minutes of morning sunlight exposure sets your circadian clock, boosts serotonin (daytime) and melatonin (nighttime). Even on cloudy days, outdoor light is 10x brighter than indoor lighting. Walk outside within an hour of waking.
